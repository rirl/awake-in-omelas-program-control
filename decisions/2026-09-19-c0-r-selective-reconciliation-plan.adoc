== Reconciliation Principle

C0-R reconciliation is performed by semantic responsibility rather than by:

* historical path;
* historical file grouping;
* branch membership;
* historical implementation mechanism.

Historical co-location is evidence of lineage, not proof of current architectural
ownership.

== Selected Execution Method

When selective reconciliation is separately authorized:

. inspect the current target canon structure;
. identify the smallest appropriate target representation by semantic responsibility;
. construct reconciled content from admitted semantic dispositions;
. preserve unresolved assertions explicitly;
. attach or record required provenance;
. compare the proposed result against both the controlled baseline and recovery
  source;
. verify rejected assertions were not introduced;
. verify historical generation tooling was not restored;
. review the complete resulting diff;
. admit only the reviewed selective result.

== Prohibited Integration Methods

The following methods are not selected:

* wholesale merge of the recovery branch;
* rebase against the recovery branch;
* indiscriminate cherry-pick sequence;
* restoration of historical paths merely because those paths existed in the
  stranded lineage;
* restoration of historical Bash generation tooling as part of canon recovery.
