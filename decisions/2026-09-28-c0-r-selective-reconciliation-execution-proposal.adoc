== Execution Principle

Selective reconciliation shall operate on semantic assertions rather than historical file identity.

For each admitted assertion:

. determine its semantic responsibility;
. identify the smallest appropriate representation in the current canon;
. preserve admitted uncertainty explicitly;
. exclude assertions rejected as current canon;
. preserve reconstructable provenance to the recovery lineage;
. avoid restoring historical decomposition merely because it existed.

A historical source file may therefore:

* contribute content to a new current canon entry;
* contribute content to an existing current canon entry;
* contribute to more than one current representation;
* be represented by another semantic structure;
* remain historical-only;
* or require no current file of its own.
