== Canon-Lineage Investigation Findings

The completed investigation established the following current findings:

* `feature/initial-canon-entries` is a valid recovery/reconciliation source.
* The post-`8b09975` lineage contains 12 linear commits ending at `8ec6666`.
* Those commits were not merged into current `development`.
* They were not cherry-picked into current `development`.
* They were not carried into current `development` as patch-equivalent changes.
* The stranded delta affects 36 paths.
* 35 of those paths are absent from current `development`.
* One path exists in evolved or different form:

----
scripts/canon-bootstrap-canon-entries-tziphora.bash
----

The investigation also established that the actual Tziphora canon content remains
byte-identical in current `development`.

The stranded material in that case is the improved generator rather than the
canon content itself.

== C0-R Governance Constraints

The following controls apply during C0-R:

* wholesale merge of `feature/initial-canon-entries` is prohibited;
* rebase of the stranded lineage into the current architecture branch is prohibited;
* indiscriminate cherry-picking of the stranded lineage is prohibited;
* selective reconciliation is required;
* reconciliation must remain reviewable;
* provenance must be preserved;
* canon content must be evaluated separately from historical tooling where their
  responsibilities differ;
* shared bootstrap/framework material must be evaluated according to current
  responsibility rather than historical co-location;
* no tooling-retention decision may be inferred merely from preserving the recovery
  lineage;
* no architectural extraction begins as part of C0-R.

== Selective Reconciliation Model

C0-R governs reconciliation by semantic responsibility rather than by historical
branch membership.

The working model is:

----
stranded lineage
      |
      +-- canon content + provenance
      |
      +-- bootstrap / framework material
      |
      +-- historical generation tooling
----

Each class is evaluated independently.

Historical co-location in Git is evidence of lineage, not proof of future
architectural ownership.

== C0-R Exit Conditions

C0-R is complete only when:

. the stranded canon lineage has been accounted for;
. the canon slices selected for reconciliation are explicitly identified;
. provenance requirements are defined for reconciled material;
. shared bootstrap/framework material has been classified for further handling;
. historical generation tooling has either:
  * an explicitly admitted disposition; or
  * an explicitly preserved unresolved decision state;
. a selective reconciliation plan has been reviewed and admitted;
. recovery evidence remains sufficient to reconstruct the source lineage;
. no wholesale integration of the recovery branch has occurred;
. the resulting program-control state explicitly authorizes transition to C1.
