== Interpretation

The whole-range `git diff --check` diagnostic was broader than the bounded
validation historically used by the governed artifact-execution work.

Existing GAE records show `git diff --check` being applied to bounded candidate
changes and staged diffs rather than establishing a repository-wide rule that
all historical content must be whitespace-clean.

Accordingly:

* the historical whitespace findings are real;
* they are not integration regressions;
* they do not invalidate the fast-forward integration;
* they should not be silently rewritten as part of parking-baseline admission;
* any cleanup should be a separate, explicitly bounded maintenance change.

This preserves provenance of previously admitted artifacts and keeps
consolidation distinct from repository hygiene work.
