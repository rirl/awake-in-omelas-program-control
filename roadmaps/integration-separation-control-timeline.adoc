=== Related Roadmap and Reference Views

The program-control repository maintains four complementary visual artifacts with distinct responsibilities:

* `integration-separation-control-timeline.png` — the established execution/control timeline. It preserves the visual lineage of the integration, parking-baseline, and bounded-remediation process and is not overwritten merely to show a later checkpoint.
* `integration-separation-control-state-m2-complete-c1-next.png` — the current control-state snapshot for the M2-complete / C1-next transition. Current-state snapshots may be added at material governance checkpoints without erasing earlier roadmap views.
* `architecture-separation-roadmap.png` — the architectural transformation roadmap. It describes the intended progression from responsibility inventory through boundary design, extraction, independent validation, and governed reintegration.
* `git-reference-timeline.png` — the historical and provenance reference. It records significant Git refs, checkpoints, branches, tags, and their relationship to repository history.

The execution/control timeline preserves roadmap-process lineage. Current-state snapshots show the latest governed checkpoint. The architectural separation roadmap describes intended architectural transformation. The Git reference timeline provides historical and provenance context.

These views are complementary and should not be treated as interchangeable. A milestone-state change does not require rewriting the architectural roadmap unless architectural intent itself changes. If execution-state information conflicts with another visual, this AsciiDoc control record remains the reviewable program-control source until the discrepancy is resolved.

== Control Principles

* Git remains the final canonical admission authority.
* Do not begin major architectural separation on `feature/openwebui-mcp-filesystem`.
* Do not begin architectural separation before B3 is closed.
* Use GAE where it materially supports the work; do not extend the GAE series merely to continue the series.
* Preserve ECR candidates without turning them automatically into scheduled work.
* Prefer clear branch and repository responsibility boundaries over long-lived entanglement.
* Treat `development` as the integration and pre-separation parking baseline for the current transition.
* Preserve observations and empirical evidence before canonizing reusable rules.
* Operational execution success does not imply artifact or change-set admissibility.
* Historical formatting debt must not be silently converted into semantic change.
* Mechanical cleanup and architectural separation are separate governed concerns.
* Admitted artifacts are not silently mutable, but they are correctable through a new governed admission event.

== Off-Path Indicators

The program should stop and reassess if any of the following occurs:

* New GAE experiments begin before M2/C1 without a clear architectural need.
* Major KIOS/Omelas separation begins on the old MCP feature branch.
* B3 expands retroactively into unrestricted repository cleanup.
* Historical or evidence semantics are changed under the label of formatting remediation.
* C1 begins before the M2 branch exists.
* Runtime responsibilities begin moving back into Omelas after separation.
* KIOS begins depending on Omelas-specific domain semantics.
* Observations are promoted directly into canon without ECR review/triage.
* Branch purpose becomes unclear or a branch begins accumulating work outside its stated responsibility.
