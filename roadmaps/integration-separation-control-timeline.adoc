== Strategic Direction

The current strategy is:

. Stop extending GAE by default.
. Close and validate the existing `feature/openwebui-mcp-filesystem` body of work.
. Perform only limited cleanup or refactoring needed to make that branch suitable for integration.
. Integrate the completed work into `development`.
. Validate `development` as a clean parking baseline.
. Create a new architecture-focused branch from the validated `development` baseline.
. Inventory current repository responsibilities.
. Define clean responsibility and dependency boundaries among Omelas, KIOS, GAE, and the governed MCP runtime.
. Separate KIOS and runtime responsibilities deliberately.
. Validate the separated components independently.
. Reintroduce KIOS into Omelas through explicit governed interfaces.
. Select additional GAE experiments only when they materially support the architectural work.

== Control Principles

* Git remains the final canonical admission authority.
* Do not begin major architectural separation on `feature/openwebui-mcp-filesystem`.
* Use GAE where it materially supports the work; do not extend the GAE series merely to continue the series.
* Preserve ECR candidates without turning them automatically into scheduled work.
* Prefer clear branch and repository responsibility boundaries over long-lived entanglement.
* Treat `development` as the integration parking baseline for the current transition.
* Preserve observations and empirical evidence before canonizing reusable rules.
* Operational execution success does not imply artifact or change-set admissibility.

== Off-Path Indicators

The program should stop and reassess if any of the following occurs:

* New GAE experiments begin before the current integration work is parked in `development` without a clear architectural need.
* Major KIOS/Omelas separation begins on the old MCP feature branch.
* The `development` validation checkpoint is skipped.
* A new branch is created before its responsibility and origin are explicit.
* Runtime responsibilities begin moving back into Omelas after separation.
* KIOS begins depending on Omelas-specific domain semantics.
* Observations are promoted directly into canon without ECR review/triage.
* Branch purpose becomes unclear or a branch begins accumulating work outside its stated responsibility.

== Checkpoint Questions

At every transition, answer these questions explicitly:

. What branch are we on?
. Is the working tree clean?
. Is the branch synchronized with its intended remote?
. Which milestone is active?
. What evidence proves the previous milestone complete?
. What is the next committed action?
. Does that action belong on the current branch?
. Are we adding work that belongs in the Potential Backlog instead?
. Are we still following the intended architectural direction?
