== Strategic Direction

The current strategy is:

. Stop extending GAE by default.
. Preserve the completed GAE-0001 through GAE-0007 evidence.
. Treat the fast-forward integration into `development` as complete.
. Treat the synchronized `development` state at `a478481` as the admitted parking baseline.
. Perform B3 — Bounded Remediation before creating the architecture/separation branch.
. Limit B3 to explicitly approved mechanical formatting corrections.
. Preserve historical and evidence semantics during B3.
. Rerun validation after any B3 change.
. Establish a cleaned pre-separation baseline if B3 changes are made.
. Create the architecture/separation branch only after B3 is complete or explicitly closed without changes.
. Begin C1 / Phase A Responsibility Inventory only on the new architecture/separation branch.
. Define clean responsibility and dependency boundaries among Omelas, KIOS, GAE, and the governed MCP runtime.
. Separate KIOS and runtime responsibilities deliberately.
. Validate the separated components independently.
. Reintroduce KIOS into Omelas through explicit governed interfaces.
. Select additional GAE experiments only when they materially support the architectural work.

=== B3 — Bounded Remediation Control Boundary

B3 exists because parking-baseline validation exposed historical formatting debt without identifying an integration regression.

Known findings:

* 28 section-spacing findings.
* 10 `git diff --check` findings.

B3 is intentionally narrow.

B3 may:

* review the known findings;
* classify which findings are safe mechanical corrections;
* correct only explicitly approved mechanical formatting issues;
* rerun the relevant rendering and whitespace validation;
* establish a cleaned pre-separation baseline if changes are made.

B3 must not:

* change narrative, governance, historical, or evidence semantics;
* rewrite admitted history;
* perform repository-wide cleanup beyond the approved scope;
* move responsibilities between Omelas, KIOS, GAE, or runtime components;
* begin architectural extraction;
* create the M2 separation branch before B3 is complete.

If review determines that no remediation should be performed, B3 may be explicitly closed without change, with that decision recorded.

== Control Principles

* Git remains the final canonical admission authority.
* Do not begin major architectural separation on `feature/openwebui-mcp-filesystem`.
* Do not begin architectural separation before B3 is closed.
* Use GAE where it materially supports the work; do not extend the GAE series merely to continue the series.
* Preserve ECR candidates without turning them automatically into scheduled work.
* Prefer clear branch and repository responsibility boundaries over long-lived entanglement.
* Treat `development` as the integration and pre-separation parking baseline for the current transition.
* Preserve observations and empirical evidence before canonizing reusable rules.
* Operational execution success does not imply artifact or change-set admissibility.
* Historical formatting debt must not be silently converted into semantic change.
* Mechanical cleanup and architectural separation are separate governed concerns.

== Off-Path Indicators

The program should stop and reassess if any of the following occurs:

* New GAE experiments begin before B3 is closed without a clear architectural need.
* Major KIOS/Omelas separation begins on the old MCP feature branch.
* B3 expands into unrestricted repository cleanup.
* Historical or evidence semantics are changed under the label of formatting remediation.
* M2 is created before B3 is completed or explicitly closed.
* C1 begins before the M2 branch exists.
* Runtime responsibilities begin moving back into Omelas after separation.
* KIOS begins depending on Omelas-specific domain semantics.
* Observations are promoted directly into canon without ECR review/triage.
* Branch purpose becomes unclear or a branch begins accumulating work outside its stated responsibility.

== Checkpoint Questions

At every transition, answer these questions explicitly:

. What branch are we on?
. Is the working tree clean?
. Is the branch synchronized with its intended remote?
. Which milestone is active?
. What evidence proves the previous milestone complete?
. What is the next committed action?
. Does that action belong on the current branch?
. Are we adding work that belongs in the Potential Backlog instead?
. Are we still following the intended architectural direction?
. If B3 is active, is every proposed change mechanical, explicitly approved, and semantics-preserving?
